A little cut. A little clever.

SNIP is a small daily word game with a satisfying twist. Cut one word out of another, and the letters left behind make a second word. Two clues. Two answers. One snip.

READ. SELECT. SNIP.
Follow the clues, drag across a continuous piece, and make your cut. You can also tap the first and last letter. Every letter has a place; no rearranging required.

YOUR DAILY THREE
Play the same dated edition as everyone else. Three puzzles make a short, thoughtful break. Daily editions change at midnight UTC, with a countdown in the app. This release includes a 30-day rotation of 90 daily puzzles.

A LITTLE MORE PLAY
Explore 12 extra puzzles in four practice collections, from First cuts to Fine cuts. Replay a collection whenever you fancy.

KEEP YOUR LITTLE RECEIPT
See your cuts, hints, and solves. Share a result card that keeps the answers secret. Track completed words, clean days, and your daily streak.

TAKE YOUR TIME
There are no timers or lives. Optional hints and reveals help you move along, and your receipt clearly records any help used.

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
Warm paper, expressive type, a crisp snip sound, and subtle haptics. Sound and haptics can be switched off. Includes larger text support, VoiceOver labels, and an alternative to dragging.

Everything works offline. Progress stays on your device. No accounts, advertisements, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. A daily reminder is available if you want one.
