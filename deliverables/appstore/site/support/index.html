How do I select a piece?

Drag across consecutive letters, or tap the first and last letter. The piece must come from inside the word. Both the cut-out and the letters left behind need at least three letters.

What counts as a cut?

A valid selection is counted when you tap Snip. Merely selecting or clearing letters costs nothing. A clean day means three first-try daily solves without hints or reveals.

What do the receipt symbols mean?

A number records your committed cuts for that word. An asterisk means a hint was used. A dash means the word was revealed; a reveal doesn't count as a solve.

When does the daily change?

At midnight UTC. Today shows a countdown in your local experience. Daily content is selected from a 30-day rotation stored in the app. If you're already playing at rollover, your current set remains available to finish.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Every included daily and practice puzzle is stored on your phone. No connection or account is needed.

Where is my progress?

It is saved on this device. Completed receipts appear in Your cuts. Replaying a practice collection keeps previous receipts. There is no cloud-sync account. Your operating system may include app data in your device backup.

How do I change reminders or reset progress?

Tap the gear in the studio. Sound, haptics, and reminders are optional. Reset my progress deletes saved results after confirmation. To change notification permission, use iPhone Settings.

Reporting an issue

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, SNIP version, and what happened. For a puzzle issue, include the edition or practice collection. A screenshot is helpful; avoid sending personal or sensitive information.
