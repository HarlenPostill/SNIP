Privacy policy for SNIP and its companion website, provided by HRLN interactive.

The SNIP app

SNIP does not collect or transmit your gameplay, device identifiers, or device information. It includes no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDKs, or tracking. It does not access your location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photo library.

Local progress and preferences

Your results, selected letters, practice sessions, and settings are saved locally on your device. The operating system may include app data in your device backup, according to your backup settings. Use Reset my progress in SNIP Settings to delete saved game results, or delete the app to remove its local data.

Reminders and sharing

Reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. Permission is requested only when you choose to enable them. SNIP does not send remote notifications.

Sharing opens the iOS share sheet. The result image and text go only to the destination you choose; nothing is shared automatically. Other apps and websites apply their own privacy policies.

Support email

If you contact support, HRLN interactive receives your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond and handle your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by contacting the address below, subject to any applicable recordkeeping requirements.

This website

These pages contain no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or forms. The website host may process basic connection information, such as IP addresses and browser-request details, to deliver and protect the site. Your email service handles messages you choose to send through an email link.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app's privacy practices change. The date above identifies the current version.
