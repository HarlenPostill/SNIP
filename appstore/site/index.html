PIERAT

SNIP is a native iPhone word game that makes a little room for an aha. Cut a continuous word out of a bigger one, and the remaining letters make a second answer.

Your daily three

Play three short puzzles in the same dated edition as everyone else, or explore four practice collections. There are no timers or lives. Hints and reveals are optional, and your receipt clearly records any help used.

Includes a 30-day rotation of 90 daily puzzles and 12 practice puzzles. Everything works offline, and your progress stays on your device.

No accounts, advertisements, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Just warm paper, a crisp snip, and some good wordplay.

For iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
